Richmond, Virginia

Location

Beautiful Richmond is located on the banks of the James River, west of Norfolk and Virginia Beach.

About

Population

The City of Richmond has just over 239,000 residents, but the metropolitan area is home to well over a million people.(1)

History

The Richmond area has many interesting and emotion-evoking historic sites and museums, as well as incredibly beautiful architecture.

Sports

Richmond Flying Squirrels Baseball and college sports (VCU Rams!) are popular in Richmond.

Things To Do

  • Richmond Symphony & Symphony Chorus concerts
  • The Poe Museum
  • Explore Carytown
